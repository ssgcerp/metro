AMC Contracts Management

Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) management system for Odoo 19.

Features:

  • Complete contract lifecycle management with customizable statuses
  • Flexible contract numbering format with prefix, branch code, sequential number, and year
  • Comprehensive contract information management
  • Visit scheduling and tracking
  • Financial tracking with expenses and invoicing
  • Service schedule management
  • Call-out and additional job tracking
